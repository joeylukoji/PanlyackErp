# PanlyackERP

## Platform
Web application, desktop-first with usable mobile navigation and tables.

## Stack
User-selected Go, Chi, templ, HTMX, Tailwind, PostgreSQL and FastAPI.

## Purpose
A document becomes reviewed inventory, accounting and cash operations. Go owns deterministic financial rules. The user explicitly requested the complete MVP flow.

## Users
Assumption from the blueprint: a small-business operator records supplier purchases and sales during daily operations.

## Constraints
English source code and documentation. French and English interfaces with a persistent language preference across the public site, authentication and workspace. User-entered business data is never translated. Clear simulated-data labeling. No automatically posted AI results. Registration creates an isolated demonstration organization.

## Public pricing
Monthly USD plans displayed on the landing page: Essential at $49 for up to 200 invoices, Growth at $79 for up to 500 invoices, and Unlimited at $150 for unlimited invoices. The calls to action open registration. Billing and quota enforcement are not implemented by this presentation change.
