# PanlyackERP workspace

Mode: Operate. A daily document-review workspace, not a marketing page.

The dashboard takes its layout cues from the MIT-licensed [StoreNG template](https://github.com/Likeur/draftng/tree/main/templates/storeng): a restrained white navigation rail, neutral work surface, compact top bar, separate KPI cards, a two-panel overview and a recent-records table. The Go/templ/HTMX application retains its own data, routes and icon sprite; no Angular UI or sample analytics are included. On phones a sticky brand bar opens a full-height navigation drawer with a backdrop and close control. Blue identifies navigation and dashboard actions; green identifies posted state, amber marks review/demo notices, and red is reserved for actionable errors.

Aptos/Segoe UI keeps dense forms familiar. Amounts use tabular numerals. Table headers are quiet, row separators aid scanning, and activity values align across a two-column ledger. Tables scroll inside their container; forms stack below 760px; document preview stacks above review below 1150px. Focus has a distinct blue outline. Inputs have labels, errors use alerts and posting success uses a status region.

The signature interaction is the document-to-ledger path: original document alongside editable extraction, explicit confirmation, then atomic posting and visible balances. Posted invoice fields are disabled; a separate settlement form shows the outstanding balance and records later payments. The dashboard shows real KPI cards, linked destinations, accounting balances, a compact photographic workflow card and recent invoices. Its mobile table keeps reference, status and amount visible. Login and registration share a split composition: a quiet form and a photographic worktable panel describing the import, review and posting steps. Registration asks only for organization name, email and password with confirmation. On phones the image becomes a compact banner above the form. Motion is limited to the navigation drawer, one photo arrival and a workflow-image hover response; reduced-motion preferences suppress spatial movement. No decorative charts, invented prices or fabricated activity are shown. The React pricing reference was used only as visual context because this product has no defined plans or prices.

The four user-supplied images are bundled as local static assets: the handoff image on login and registration, the green growth image on the dashboard workflow card, the analytics-hand image beside invoice import, and the gold graph above the accounting journal. They are decorative; product copy and real records carry the meaning. The synthetic invoice PNG remains labeled as test data. The replaced generated photographs were removed from the bundle.

The user-supplied monochrome P logo is bundled as panlyack-logo.png and used in authentication, navigation, the workspace badge and the favicon. The original image is preserved; clipped containers frame its whitespace at small sizes. Adjacent brand text provides the accessible name.
