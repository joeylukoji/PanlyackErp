# PanlyackERP workspace

Mode: Operate. A daily document-review workspace, not a marketing page.

The dashboard retains the existing white sidebar and compact top bar, while its content follows the user's supplied finance-dashboard screenshot: two large balance/revenue cards followed by spending, outstanding amounts and recent invoices. A pale neutral work surface, white rounded cards and the landing page's lime accent (#d2ef7a) establish the shared identity. The Go/templ/HTMX application retains its own organization-scoped data, routes and icon sprite. On phones a sticky brand bar opens the existing navigation drawer with a backdrop and close control. Green identifies navigation and primary actions; amber marks review/demo notices and red remains reserved for actionable errors.

Self-hosted Manrope matches the landing page across authentication and the workspace. Amounts use tabular numerals. Table headers are quiet and row separators aid scanning. Tables scroll inside their container; forms stack below 760px; document preview stacks above review below 1150px. Focus has a distinct dark-green outline. Inputs have labels, errors use alerts and posting success uses a status region.

The signature interaction is the document-to-ledger path: original document alongside editable extraction, explicit confirmation, then atomic posting and visible balances. Posted invoice fields are disabled; a separate settlement form shows the outstanding balance and records later payments. The dashboard displays real cash, revenue, stock, posted count, purchases, expenses, receivables, payables and cost figures. The spending ring represents the share of purchases in purchases plus expenses, calculated with decimal arithmetic. It remains neutral for zero, invalid or negative component values. The existing HTMX endpoint refreshes all summary cards every 30 seconds; recent invoice links remain the page-load snapshot. Up to four recent invoices show their original currency, date and state, with a link to the full list. No invented trends or notification counts are included.

Login and registration use a quiet ivory form and rounded navy/teal photographic panel with lime typography and the same primary buttons as the landing. Registration retains organization name, email, password and confirmation; field names, CSRF tokens, validation constraints and submission routes are unchanged. The logo links back to the public landing. On phones the story panel becomes a compact banner above the form. Motion is limited to the existing navigation drawer and photo arrival; reduced-motion preferences suppress spatial movement.

The four user-supplied images are bundled as local static assets: the handoff image on login and registration, the green growth image on the public landing, the analytics-hand image beside invoice import, and the gold graph above the accounting journal. They are decorative; product copy and real records carry the meaning. The synthetic invoice PNG remains labeled as test data. The replaced generated photographs were removed from the bundle.

The user-supplied monochrome P logo is bundled as panlyack-logo.png and used in authentication, navigation, the workspace badge and the favicon. The original image is preserved; clipped containers frame its whitespace at small sizes. Adjacent brand text provides the accessible name.

The accounting screen presents a trial balance above the journal. Account codes link directly to their ledger lines; a compact selector allows switching accounts or returning to all entries. Wide financial tables scroll within their panels on small screens.

## Public landing page

The public `/` route uses Persuade mode, separate from the workspace. Its composition takes the supplied HTML's product-led sections and the supplied financial-page screenshot's navy/teal surfaces and lime accent. Self-hosted Manrope provides its display and body typography; `landing.css` is isolated from workspace styles. The actual Panlyack logo and existing growth photograph are reused. The hero invoice is explicitly illustrative, with no fabricated customer counts, reviews or performance claims. Feature descriptions cover implemented invoice, inventory, accounting and payment flows. Native `details` provides the mobile hamburger and FAQ without a JavaScript dependency; all sign-in and registration links lead to `/login` and `/register`. Reduced-motion preferences disable the single product entrance. The footer and FAQ identify the local demonstration scope.

## Verification of authentication/workspace alignment

Generated templ sources and Tailwind assets, Go tests, vet and the ERP build succeeded. Browser checks covered desktop and 390px mobile: loaded Manrope, exact button color rgb(210,239,122), authentication cross-links, required inputs/CSRF field preservation, dashboard empty state, no horizontal page overflow, hamburger opening/closing and no console errors on the inspected authentication page. Decimal spending calculations cover zero, negative, invalid and fractional inputs. The Impeccable detector returned no findings.

Docker's pre-existing local inference socket failure still prevents the full PostgreSQL-backed app from starting. Browser checks therefore used the existing isolated preview server on port8097, with authentication GET routes and explicitly labelled fictitious dashboard fixtures under `/preview/dashboard`. Preview fixtures and the preview route are not included in the ERP binary. Authentication submission and database-backed dashboard data were not revalidated in this session.
